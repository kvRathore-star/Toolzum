TOOLZUM

Brand Guidelines — Final Logo System · v2.1 (Indigo/Dark) · 2026

01 — Primary Mark

The Toolzum mark is a single continuous tapered stroke forming an abstract "Z" gesture — representing precision, speed, and a signal path. Rebuilt from the original v1.0 blue system onto a vivid indigo accent, matching the brand equity already established by the original wordmark. This colorway carries more personality and stands out further from the sea of blue-branded privacy/SaaS competitors, while keeping the same abstract, non-literal, technology-agnostic mark so it continues to age well.

Stroke weight increased proportionally at 32px and 16px to prevent the mark from thinning out and disappearing at browser-tab scale.

03 — Wordmark

Custom-set wordmark restoring the original Tool/zum split-color treatment: "Tool" in off-white, "zum" in brand indigo (#6366F1) — same color logic as the original pre-icon lockup, now paired with the finalized tapered-Z icon.

04 — Primary Lockup (Icon + Wordmark)

Primary lockup — dark background. Website header, app UI, splash screens. This is the default, matching the original brand equity.

Light background variant — print, light-mode UI, partner co-branding. Purple darkened slightly for AA contrast on white.

On-brand-color background — social banners, merch, presentation title slides.

05 — Clear Space & Minimum Size

Minimum clear space around the mark = the height of the "Z" stroke itself (dashed border above). Never place text, other logos, or UI elements inside this zone. Minimum reproduction size: 16px digital / 8mm print — below this, use the icon-only mark without the wordmark.

Dark background is now the primary/default presentation, matching original brand equity. Light and full-color variants exist for contexts that require them (print, partner logos, co-branding).

07 — Incorrect Usage

✕ Do not recolor the badge
✕ Do not rotate or flip
✕ Do not thin the stroke
✕ Do not square off corners

08 — Digital Small-Scale Exception (Sep 2026)

The live product (header, footer, UI accents) and social cards render the wordmark "zum" in #818CF8 instead of #6366F1. Reason: #6366F1 falls below comfortable contrast on near-black at small sizes and across themes; #818CF8 is the app's --accent everywhere users look (buttons, links, wordmark). The icon badge fill stays #6366F1 in all contexts. One identity on screen beats paper truth — this exception keeps them one.
